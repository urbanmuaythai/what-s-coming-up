THAILAND TOUR 2026 · RECAP

We came. We trained. We conquered.

The Urban crew has wrapped two unforgettable weeks in Thailand. Complete beginners, experienced members and four fighters trained together, explored together and came home with new skills, friendships and stories.

Every session included five rounds of one-on-one training, with around 20 Thai trainers sharing different styles, knowledge and experience with the group.

Two Thailand fights. Two wins.
Cooper brought home the Sinbi Boxing Stadium belt, and Aanya earned a victory in only her second fight.

The latest reel captures what made the tour special beyond the training: the laughs, adventures and connections that turned training partners into lifelong friends. Thank you to everyone who represented Urban so proudly. The 2027 tour dates are coming soon.
