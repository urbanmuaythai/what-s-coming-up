Four big ones this month. Mark the calendar, grab a partner, and chat to the front desk to lock in your spot.

Saturday · 15 August · 8:00 AM Workshop

Pad Flow Workshop

Our Pad Flow Workshop is built for anyone who struggles with calling freestyle pads — but it's open to everybody, regardless of experience. It's a partner-based class focused on developing smooth, effective pad holding and striking.

In this session you'll learn to
  • Link combinations together naturally
  • Call and flow combinations effectively
  • Transition between all eight weapons of Muay Thai
  • Improve timing, communication and fight IQ
  • Build confidence as both a striker and a pad holder

Whether you're new to pad holding or looking to sharpen up, this workshop will make you a more complete training partner.

Friday · 21 August · 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM Social

Spar, Slices & Suds

Our in-club sparring and social event! The sparring session is open to all levels — including kids, teens and advanced students. After training, stick around to relax with your teammates, enjoy some pizza and get to know everyone around the club.

What to expect
  • In-club sparring for all experience levels
  • Pizza provided
  • BYO drinks
  • A fun, relaxed social atmosphere
  • Meet new teammates and enjoy time with the community
