WRAP-UP

Event
recaps.

Missed one, or want to relive it? Here's how the Urban Army's recent events went down.

Wrapped · 31 August 2026Grading recap

Teens Blue Grading

Our teens closed out August by stepping up for Blue Grading and putting their hard-earned skills on display. They showed focus, composure and the confidence that comes from consistent training. A strong effort from everyone who took on the challenge.

Wrapped · 29 August 2026Fight show recap

Destiny Fight Show

Chelsea delivered her best performance yet, showing six months of growth through sharper angles, committed attacks and a clear mind under pressure. Mitch stepped in on late notice for only his 10th fight, faced an opponent with 50 fights and produced a brilliant victory that has promoters taking notice. Two performances that made the whole Urban team proud.

View the full fight recap
Wrapped · 22 August 2026 Grading recap

Adult Blue Grading

Adult Blue Grading ran brilliantly, with seven members stepping up for their Blue Singlets. Everyone pushed the pace and showcased the skills they have developed throughout their training. Their hard work paid off: all seven completed the grading and earned their Blue Singlets. A huge achievement and a well-deserved result for the whole group.

Wrapped · 21 August 2026 Social recap

Spar, Slices & Suds

Spar, Slices & Suds was a huge success, with more than 50 people joining us and 48 jumping in for sparring. The energy was high, the tunes were pumping and the rounds kept flowing. Afterwards, the Urban crew kicked back over well-earned pizza and beers, catching up with friends and making new ones. With so many people already asking when the next one is, there is only one answer: Spar, Slices & Suds will return!

Wrapped · 15 August 2026 Workshop recap

Pad Flow Workshop

The Urban Army turned out for a focused morning of better pad holding, smoother combinations and sharper communication. Members worked with their training partners to call freestyle pads with more confidence and flow through all eight weapons of Muay Thai.

  • Combinations linked together more naturally
  • Stronger timing between strikers and pad holders
  • Smoother transitions across all eight weapons
  • More confident, complete training partners

Winter Weapon 2026

Our biggest winter challenge yet. Winter Weapon was all about staying consistent, keeping each other accountable and continuing to show up through the colder months — and the Army delivered. Huge effort from everyone who chased down a reward tier.
