Urban Muaythai
Beach Olympics.

A fun, friendly competition for all Urban Muaythai members to challenge themselves, support their teammates and enjoy a day at the beach. Choose your events, train through September and put your preparation to the test.

Choose your
challenges.

The Olympics features 12 events across four categories. Each event is scored separately, and individual points combine to decide the overall Urban Muay Thai Beach Olympics Champion.

Speed events

  1. Beach Sprint · IndividualRace 40 metres on soft sand.
  2. Relay Run · Team of fourComplete a 30 metre by four shuttle relay.
  3. Beach Obstacle Sprint · IndividualComplete a 10 metre sprint, bear crawl, cone weave and sprint finish.

Strength events

  1. Push-Up Challenge · IndividualComplete as many push-ups as possible in two minutes.
  2. Sit-Up Challenge · IndividualComplete as many sit-ups as possible in two minutes.
  3. Water Bucket Relay · Team of fourCarry water in buckets to fill a container.

Muay Thai events

  1. Teep Challenge · IndividualWith a partner wearing a belly pad, complete as many teeps and push kicks as possible in two minutes.
  2. Kick Challenge · IndividualWith a pad holder, complete as many alternating left and right kicks as possible in two minutes.
  3. Punch Challenge · IndividualComplete as many rounds as possible in two minutes: 10 straight punches, 10 uppercuts and 10 hooks.

Fun events

  1. Tug of War · Team of sixFace other teams in a single-elimination tournament.
  2. Three-Legged Relay · Team of twoRace together while your inside legs are tied.
  3. Partner Wheelbarrow Race · Team of twoWork as a pair to race to the finish.

How points
are scored.

Points are awarded in every event and added together at the end of the day. Medals go to the top three in each event before the overall champion is crowned.

Gold10 points
Silver7 points
Bronze5 points
Participation2 points

How the day
will run.

Events run one at a time. Coaches will explain and demonstrate every challenge before it begins, call competitors when it is their turn and keep the day moving safely.

  1. Arrival and check-inConfirm the events you have registered for.
  2. Welcome and briefingHear the rules, scoring, safety information and event order.
  3. Group warm-upEveryone prepares together before competition begins.
  4. Speed eventsBeach Sprint, Relay Run and Beach Obstacle Sprint.
  5. Strength eventsPush-Ups, Sit-Ups and Water Bucket Relay.
  6. Muay Thai eventsTeep, Kick and Punch Challenges.
  7. Fun eventsTug of War, Three-Legged Relay and Partner Wheelbarrow Race.
  8. Results and presentationScores are totalled, then medals, the champion trophy and additional awards are presented.
  9. BBQ and social timeRelax, grab some food and enjoy time with the Urban community.

September
preparation.

Olympics training is built into September classes so members can learn every event, improve their scores and experience competition conditions before the big day.

7–10 September

Week 1 · Build the foundation

Learn the events and establish your personal benchmark.

  • Monday: speed and 40 metre sprint technique
  • Tuesday: strength tests and bucket carries
  • Wednesday: Muay Thai challenges
  • Thursday: team and fun events
14–17 September

Week 2 · Improve technique

Become more efficient and aim to beat your Week 1 scores.

  • Monday: sprint starts
  • Tuesday: strength pacing, breathing and longer carries
  • Wednesday: teep, kick and punch efficiency
  • Thursday: mini team-event tournament
21–24 September

Week 3 · Competition week

Run mini competitions under pressure and build confidence.

  • Monday: speed events
  • Tuesday: strength events
  • Wednesday: Muay Thai events
  • Thursday: fun events
28 September–1 October

Week 4 · Refine your skills

Choose the event you most want to improve and work on it at the start of class.

  • Monday to Thursday
  • 10 minutes of focused event practice
  • Use your earlier scores to guide your final preparation

Register and
choose your events.

Members register before competition day and choose the events they want to enter. If you are entering a team challenge, organise your teammates ahead of the event so your team is ready to go.
