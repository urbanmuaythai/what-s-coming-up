Underway · 2026

Training. Adventure.
Two Thailand wins.

The tour runs from 23 October to 6 November. So far, the crew has shared training at 7.30am and 4pm every day (Thailand time), padwork, learning the Wai Kru, kayaking and meals together. On 31 October, Cooper and Aanya added two fight wins to the trip.

Two wins in Thailand.

Cooper claimed the Sinbi Boxing Stadium belt against an opponent with ten fights’ experience. Aanya also earned a victory in only her second fight, showing composure and putting her corner’s advice into action. Back in Australia, Mitch won by first-round TKO at YOKKAO — three wins for Urban across the two countries.
