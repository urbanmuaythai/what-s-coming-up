Fight recap

Chelsea
vs Houston

No win · Plenty learned

Opening the show, Chelsea delivered the best performance of her career so far. Richie saw a huge improvement: she used her angles, committed to her attacks and, most importantly, stayed clear-minded enough to read the fight as it unfolded.

“I was super proud of the aggression and power I brought, and I came away knowing exactly what I need to work on for next time.”

Chelsea did not get the win, with Houston proving stronger in the clinch during the final round. Even so, the fight showed just how far Chelsea has come over the last six months. Her composure, awareness and willingness to attack gave the team plenty to celebrate and a clear foundation to build on.

After her fight, Chelsea stepped up as the ringside doctor's assistant, helping stitch up multiple fighters throughout the night. Even with her own bout finished, she stayed fully involved and supported the people around her. Her message afterwards was clear: this is definitely not the last time we will see her in the ring.

Fight recap

Mitch
steps up

Victory · A talent to watch

Mitch accepted the fight on late notice after Harry was ruled out with a broken nose. It was only Mitch's 10th fight, and across the ring stood a seasoned campaigner entering his 50th—a formidable test and a major opportunity to step up.

Mitch took that opportunity with both hands. He showcased the sharp Muay Thai skills the team has watched him build in recent weeks and produced a fantastic victory against a vastly more experienced opponent.

“It was a great victory, but more than that, Mitch showcased some fantastic Muay Thai skills. He is definitely a talent to look out for.” — Richie

The performance made an immediate impression. Promoters have already been reaching out to match Mitch on future shows, and the whole team is incredibly proud of the skill, courage and professionalism with which he represented Urban Muaythai.
