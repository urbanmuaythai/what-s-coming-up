Our mission

To empower people to live a better life through the “Magic of Muaythai”.

01

How we show up.

Every day. Non-negotiable. This is what it means to be part of Urban Muaythai.

01

Take ownership

We make it happen, taking responsibility for our role, our actions and our standards. We don’t wait to be told. We care deeply about the outcome and give 100% effort.

02

Passion from the heart

We coach, lead and support with genuine care. Our energy, enthusiasm and love for Muay Thai are felt the moment someone walks through the door. We support our teammates and our members in achieving their goals.

03

Positive growth mindset

A positive mindset is how we show up on the mats, in class and in life. We lead with energy, encouragement and a focus on what’s possible. We lift each other up, bounce back from setbacks and bring good vibes to every session. Mindset is contagious, so we keep it strong.

04

Respect

We respect our members, our teammates, our space and ourselves. We take pride in our appearance, environment and behaviour. Respect isn’t expected. It’s lived every day.

05

Welcoming & community driven

Everyone belongs here. From day one, people feel seen, supported and included. A place to truly belong, somewhere to switch off from the world and feel celebrated. We know your name, we connect with your story, and we fist pump every class.

06

“Fuck” fine

We don’t aim to be average. We strive to be great in coaching, culture, systems and experience.

02

The U.R.B.A.N Method.

Help every member get fit and healthy through Muay Thai, within a welcoming, supportive community. Build energy, confidence and a new zest for life that extends far beyond the mats.

U

Understanding the client

Customer service means building rapport and understanding each member’s needs and goals.

The outcome: A welcoming, supportive environment where members feel comfortable and part of the community.

R

Results

Fit and healthy. Increased fitness and transformation of body and mind.

The outcome: The tools to change small daily habits and set up progression and success.

B

Benefits

Class structures that create progression, feedback and the winning feeling.

The outcome: Increased confidence in Muay Thai and in life.

A

Accountability

A community of friends who keep each other accountable and coming back to training.

The outcome: Urban becomes a second family, where members feel part of the Urban Army.

N

New you

The Magic of Muaythai and its life-changing effects.

Outcomes for members · The longer journey
2–6 months

A significant shift in fitness. Yellow grading complete. Starting to develop a core connection with Muay Thai and become part of our community. #urbanarmy.

6–12 months

Blue and possibly green grading completed. A noticeable difference in mindset and physical fitness. A core part of the Urban Army.

1–2 years

Green and purple grading completed. A desire to compete and a first fight. Leaders in the gym, with Muay Thai a way of life. Social and encouraging others to become part of the Urban Army.

2–3 years

Black singlet grading. Members may become interested in instructing or start to become fighters.

3 years +

Opportunities for a role within the gym. Black singlet grading. Social club leaders and fighters.
